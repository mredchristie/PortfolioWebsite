Live from my own API water.mredchristie.dev (opens in new tab)

I am building one data source for the Cornish coast: the beaches worth knowing, which are good for snorkelling, and the sewage discharge record for each of them, with tide and water quality to follow. The official feed keeps no history, so a collector running on Cloudflare Workers writes it down every 15 minutes into D1, alongside six years of audited Environment Agency returns. It is all served as a public API that anyone can query.

Storm overflow data from South West Water (CC BY 4.0) and the Environment Agency (OGL v3.0). Live figures are not audited and this is not advice about whether it is safe to swim.
